# Debugging and hotfixes

Reproduce safely where feasible and trace root cause, affected consumers, and related paths. Double-check relevant context and counterexamples before reporting a cause as confirmed. Label conclusions Confirmed, Likely, or Unverified and cite evidence. Never claim certainty beyond the available evidence. Propose and obtain approval for the fix and relevant regression checks. Verify the fix and assess existing data repair and post-release monitoring separately.
